Privacy Policy

ZH Workforce Innovation

ZH Workforce Innovation respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how employee data is handled when using the app.

Information Collection

ZH Workforce Innovation does not automatically collect personal data. All information is provided directly by the user (employee) when creating or updating their profile.

Users may provide the following data as part of their employee profile:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Job title
  • Role
  • Profile photo

Use of Information

Data is used only to create and manage the employee's profile within the workplace system, and to enable the app's core functionality (such as identifying the employee within their workplace).

Data Collection & Analytics

ZH Workforce Innovation does not use any third-party analytics or crash reporting tools. No personal data is used for advertising or tracking purposes.

Data Sharing

ZH Workforce Innovation does not sell or share personal data with third parties. Employee data is only accessible within the workplace system it belongs to.

Data Storage

All data is stored securely and is only maintained as long as necessary to provide app functionality.

Data Deletion

Users can permanently delete their account and all associated data at any time from within the app (Account section).

User Control

Users have full control over their information and can edit or update it at any time from their profile.

Children's Privacy

ZH Workforce Innovation does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If such data is found, it will be deleted immediately.

Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect user data against unauthorized access or misuse.
